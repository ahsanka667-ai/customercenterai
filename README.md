# CustomerCenter AI

**AI order confirmation for Facebook-first B2B sellers — in the seller's language, under the seller's brand.**

**Read the plan: [PLAN.md](./PLAN.md)** (v0.2)

The short version:

- **B2B SaaS:** you sell to sellers; the AI calls their buyers. The seller configures language, voice, brand name, script, hours, and confirmation mode.
- **Facebook is not a phone channel.** Messenger is intake + in-window chat; the confirmation call runs over telephony. No outbound calling API, no phone number in the profile, and App Review is the long pole.
- **Messenger first:** order extraction → chat confirmation card → call escalation → structured outcome.
- **Language is a product setting:** Bangla + English first; new markets are language packs + local telephony, not rewrites.
- **Cost is the constraint:** chat-first escalation, local SIP, 45–90s calls, small models. The managed voice stack is for the pilot, not the business model.
- **Start Meta Business Verification and App Review in week 1** while building the voice engine and website/CSV intake.

Status: planning / pre-MVP.
